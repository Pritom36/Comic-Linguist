Vocabulary Lists

Common Comic Book Vocabulary

Character Descriptions

  • Hero A brave or exceptional person, especially one who helps others "Every hero needs a weakness to overcome."
  • Villain A character whose evil actions or motives are important to the plot "The villain revealed their master plan."
  • Sidekick A close friend or associate who helps and supports someone "Every hero needs a reliable sidekick."

Action Words

  • Battle To fight or struggle against someone or something "The heroes battle against evil forces."
  • Defend To protect from harm or danger "They defend the city from attacks."
  • Transform To change completely in form or appearance "The character transforms into their superhero form."

Essential Expressions

Common Phrases

  • Look out! Used to warn someone of danger "Look out! The building is falling!"
  • Never give up To continue trying, despite difficulties "Even when things look bad, never give up!"
  • Count on me To depend on or trust someone "You can count on me to help!"

Exclamations

  • Amazing! Expressing great surprise or wonder "Amazing! They defeated the monster!"
  • Oh no! Expressing dismay or concern "Oh no! The villain escaped!"
  • Incredible! Expressing disbelief or amazement "Incredible! Their power level is off the charts!"

Descriptive Language

Appearance Words

  • Muscular Having well-developed muscles; strong "The muscular hero lifted the car easily."
  • Mysterious Difficult to understand or explain; enigmatic "A mysterious figure appeared in the shadows."
  • Agile Able to move quickly and easily "The agile hero dodged all attacks."

Setting Description

  • Gloomy Dark or dim; expressing melancholy "The gloomy alley was perfect for an ambush."
  • Bustling Full of energetic activity "The bustling city never sleeps."
  • Desolate Empty and lonely; deserted "The battlefield was desolate after the fight."
