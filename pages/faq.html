Frequently Asked Questions

Getting Started

How do I start learning with Comic Linguist?

To get started:

  1. Take our level assessment test
  2. Choose comics that match your level
  3. Start reading and using our interactive tools
  4. Track your progress and practice regularly

What level should I start at?

We recommend taking our quick placement test to determine your starting level. The test evaluates your:

  • Reading comprehension
  • Vocabulary knowledge
  • Grammar understanding
  • Language comfort level

Using Comic Linguist

How do the interactive features work?

Our interactive features include:

  • Click-to-translate words
  • Audio pronunciation
  • Grammar explanations
  • Practice exercises
  • Progress tracking

Simply click or tap on any word or phrase to access these features.

Can I save my progress?

Yes! Comic Linguist automatically saves your:

  • Reading progress
  • Vocabulary lists
  • Exercise results
  • Achievement badges

Your progress syncs across all your devices when you're logged in.

Comics and Content

What types of comics are available?

We offer a wide variety of genres:

  • Adventure
  • Superhero
  • Slice of Life
  • Mystery
  • Fantasy
  • Educational

All comics are carefully selected and adapted for language learners.

How often is new content added?

We regularly update our content:

  • New comics added weekly
  • Monthly vocabulary updates
  • Regular grammar lessons
  • New practice exercises

Technical Support

Which devices can I use?

Comic Linguist works on:

  • Desktop computers
  • Laptops
  • Tablets
  • Smartphones

Our responsive design ensures a great experience on any device.

How do I report issues?

To report issues:

  1. Visit our Help Center
  2. Use the contact form
  3. Email support@comiclinguist.com
  4. Use in-app reporting tools

Our support team typically responds within 24 hours.
